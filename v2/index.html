Environmental control

Read the short version

I work between engineering and regulatory evidence: I write the protocols, run the studies, close the CAPAs and deviations, and then defend the documents when FDA comes in. Sterile and 503B compounding operations are unforgiving about detail, which is the part I like.

Before this I was the site validation engineer at Hikma's 503B outsourcing facility, and before that a graduate engineering intern there while I finished my M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Rutgers.
